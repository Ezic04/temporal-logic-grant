
**Task 1.** Construction of infinitary systems for the logics $\UB$ and $\CTL$ with the strong completeness property in tree structures with finite branching.

**Task 2.** Construction of infinitary systems for the logics $\TLR$, $\UB$, and $\CTL$ with the strong completeness property in the class of all infinite trees.

**Task 3.** Construction of infinitary systems for the logic $\CTLs$ and selected sublogics thereof possessing the strong completeness property in the semantics of infinite trees.

**Task 4.** Philosophical justification of the usefulness of systems formulated in Tasks 1-3 based on dispositional conceptions of belief and an examination of their possible applications in contemporary philosophical discourse on time.
